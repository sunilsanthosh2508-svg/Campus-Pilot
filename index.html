THE PROBLEM

Too many tools.
One project.

College projects often mean switching between documents, AI tools, design tools, GitHub, presentation software, and scattered notes.

Campus Pilot is exploring a simpler way to organize that journey—without forcing students to rebuild their workflow from scratch.
